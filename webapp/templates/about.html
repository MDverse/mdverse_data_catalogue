What is MDverse?

And what it is not.

MDverse is the data catalogue of molecular dynamics (MD) simulations. It indexes, annotates, and shares datasets and files related to MD simulations with the purpose of fostering dissemination and reuse.

MDverse is not a data repository and does not host any MD data. It works like a search engine with added value for the MD community.

MDverse metadata will be integrated into the discovery platform developed by the EOSC LUMEN project.

Who is running MDverse?

MDverse was created by Johanna Tiemann, Matthieu Chavent, and Pierre Poulain. See original publication: MDverse, shedding light on the dark matter of molecular dynamics simulations , eLife, 2024 (DOI: 10.7554/eLife.90061.3).

📝 The “Dark Matter of Molecular Dynamics Simulations” refers to data that is technically accessible, but neither indexed, curated, nor easily searchable.

MDverse is now maintained and operated by Matthieu Chavent and Pierre Poulain.
